# Copy standards

## Register

Compressed newspaper prose. Specific nouns. Active verbs. Mildly opinionated about what is interesting, never personal about who is reading.

## Required item shape

- concrete headline;
- one-sentence deck;
- source/date/type line;
- a short source-grounded summary;
- a distinct desk note for editorial synthesis;
- at least one direct source or deep-reading link;
- uncertainty preserved when evidence is incomplete or contested.

## Prohibited habits

No reader-directed advice, personal-feed language, simulated first-person voice, inflated certainty, generic AI filler, clickbait, or score-based recommendation language. Do not use a source title as evidence for claims that only appear in an abstract, thread, or secondary report.
