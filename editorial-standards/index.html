What gets printed

Source before flourish

Every dispatch names its source, date, and direct reading link. Summaries stay within the evidence available from the source. When a claim is moving, disputed, or thin, the language says so.

Compressed, not flattened

The desk reduces a piece to its useful shape without pretending that a paper, a discussion thread, a podcast, and a personal essay are the same kind of object.

Public by construction

The public edition contains no private-feed labels, saved-note metadata, personal reactions, account identity, or reader profiling. Selection can happen privately; publication remains neutral.

Copy rules

  • Concrete headlines; no clickbait or inflated certainty.
  • No generic filler such as “this article explores” or “game-changing.”
  • No advice addressed to a reader and no simulated personal voice.
  • Short quotations remain exact and traceable.
  • Editorial synthesis is kept distinct from source fact.
  • Every item needs a deep link, not only a headline.

Evidence marks

checked means the desk has a primary source or paper. reported means the item is a public report or feed signal. contested, moving, and thin preserve uncertainty rather than hiding it.
